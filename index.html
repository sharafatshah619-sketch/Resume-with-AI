How to Make a Professional Resume

A professional resume must succinctly summarize your qualifications, career history, education, and competencies. Start with a prominent header featuring your contact details and LinkedIn profile. Follow with a 3–4 sentence professional summary highlighting your specialization. Structure your work experience with reverse-chronological bullet points that quantify achievements.

What is an ATS-Friendly Resume?

Over 95% of Fortune 500 companies utilize Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) to filter job applications before human recruiters ever see them. An ATS-friendly resume avoids complex tables, icons, and non-standard fonts. It utilizes standard headings (e.g. Work Experience, Education) and incorporates target keywords found directly within the job posting.

How to Write a Resume Summary

Your resume summary is the elevator pitch at the top of the page. Replace outdated "Objective" statements with a targeted summary that defines who you are, your years of experience, core hard skills, and an exemplary measurable achievement. ResumeAI Pro's AI generator creates tailored summaries instantly based on your target job title.

How to Create a Resume for a Fresh Graduate

Entry-level candidates often lack extensive full-time experience. In this scenario, spotlight your educational background, capstone projects, academic honors, technical proficiencies, internships, and relevant certifications. Our "Fresh Graduate" template is structured specifically to emphasize academic foundations and project deliverables.
