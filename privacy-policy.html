Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

ResumeAI Pro is architected with a privacy-first mindset. When you build a resume using our web application:

  • Resume Form Data: Information you type (e.g. name, work experience, phone, skills) is saved primarily in your web browser's local cache (LocalStorage).
  • AI Generation Requests: When you click an AI enhancement button, the relevant prompt text is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS to our secure backend proxy solely to generate the requested summary or bullet points. We do not use your personal information to train public AI foundation models.
  • Contact Inquiries: If you contact us via our contact form, we collect your name, email, and message to respond to your support request.

2. How We Store Your Data

Your working drafts remain in your client-side web browser. You can clear or remove your saved resume at any time simply by clicking the "Clear" button in the resume builder or by clearing your browser's site data.

3. Cookies and Analytics

ResumeAI Pro does not utilize invasive tracking cookies or sell your browsing activity to third-party ad networks. We may utilize standard server logs or anonymized measurement scripts to monitor website uptime, performance, and error diagnostics.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application interacts with trusted cloud infrastructure and AI processing APIs (e.g. Google Gemini API) solely to execute server-side prompt enhancements. These providers are bound by strict enterprise confidentiality protocols.

5. Contact Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions or data requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact Page or email privacy@resumeai-pro.example.com.
