Step 1: Personal Information

Ensure your contact details are accurate so recruiters can reach you easily.

Note: Most ATS parsers recommend omitting photos unless requested in specific countries.

Step 2: Professional Summary

A brief 3-4 sentence elevator pitch highlighting your key strengths and readiness to deliver value.

Tip: Mention your years of experience, primary technical skills, and a quantifiable achievement.

Step 3: Education

List your degrees, diplomas, high school, or vocational coursework in reverse-chronological order.

Step 4: Work Experience

Detail your roles and achievements. Use the AI button to improve bullet points with action verbs.

Step 5: Skills

Add targeted skills and keywords that match the job description you want to apply for.

Step 6: Languages

List languages you speak and your level of proficiency.

Step 7: Certifications & Licenses

Add professional certifications, security credentials, state licenses, or technical badges.

Step 8: Projects

Highlight key academic, open source, or workplace initiatives and technological solutions.

Step 9: References (Optional)

You may add references or simply state "Available upon request" according to job requirements.

Step 10: Choose Your ATS Template

Pick from our 5 recruiter-approved templates. Your data adapts automatically.

Modern Professional
